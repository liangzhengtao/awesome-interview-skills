# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| Latest | ✅ |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

This project contains educational content (markdown files) and does not process user data or execute code. Therefore, traditional security vulnerabilities do not apply.

However, if you find:

- **Inaccurate information** that could mislead interviewees
- **Outdated advice** that could harm someone's interview performance
- **Plagiarized content** from copyrighted sources
- **Offensive or inappropriate content**

Please report it by:

1. Opening a [GitHub Issue](../../issues) with the `security` label
2. Or emailing the maintainer directly

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgment**: Within 48 hours
- **Assessment**: Within 1 week
- **Resolution**: Within 2 weeks (depending on severity)

## Content Accuracy

We strive to provide accurate, up-to-date interview advice. Interview practices evolve, and we welcome corrections from:

- Current and former interviewers at tech companies
- Recruiters and hiring managers
- Engineers who have recently gone through the interview process

Thank you for helping maintain the quality of this project.
